# Voice — Maya Okonkwo

> Fictional. This is a worked example of a v0.1 `voice.md` for a developer
> early in her career who hasn't yet developed strong stylistic preferences.

## Register

Default mode: friendly-direct. Slightly formal in professional Slack
because she's still feeling out the team. Comfortable in code reviews;
less comfortable in design docs.

## What I want from AI assistants

- Explain *why* an approach works, not just what to type. Building
  intuition matters more than shipping the line.
- When two patterns are both common (e.g. `if x is None:` vs
  `if not x:`), name the tradeoff so I learn the difference.
- Don't assume I've used `<framework>` before. Default to "show me the
  smallest working example" rather than "here's how to extend it."

## What to avoid

- Cargo-cult "this is the Pythonic way" without explaining why.
- Sentences like "as you already know" — often I don't.
- Stack Overflow-shaped one-liners with no surrounding context.
